How your data is handled, what rights you have, and how AI is used in the services offered by Sophfree Automation.

Who we are

About Sophfree

Sophfree Automation is a trading name of Sophie Sherwin, a sole trader registered in England and Wales. For any data-related enquiries, contact sophie@sophfree.com.

We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as required under UK data protection law.

Your data

What we collect & why

Enquiry data: When you submit the contact form on this website, we collect your name, email address, and the details you provide about your business and automation needs. This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and discuss whether we can work together.

Call recordings: Discovery calls may be recorded for the purpose of accurately understanding your requirements. You will be informed at the start of any call if it is being recorded and can decline. Recordings are not shared with third parties and are deleted once they have served their purpose.

What we do not do: We do not sell your data. We do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not use tracking scripts, advertising pixels, or analytics tools on this website.

Legal basis

UK GDPR & your rights

We process your personal data on the basis of legitimate interest — specifically, to respond to an enquiry you have initiated — and, where relevant, on the basis of contract where we are working together.

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, request its correction or deletion, object to its processing, and request that we restrict how we use it. To exercise any of these rights, contact sophie@sophfree.com and we will respond within 30 days.

If you have a concern about how your data is handled that we are unable to resolve, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office.

Data retention

How long we keep your data

Enquiry data is retained for as long as is necessary to respond to and follow up on your enquiry — typically no longer than 12 months if no working relationship is established. If we work together, data relevant to the project may be retained for up to 6 years in line with standard UK business record-keeping obligations.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing sophie@sophfree.com.

EU AI Act — Transparency statement

How AI is used in our services

Sophfree Automation builds workflow automations that may incorporate artificial intelligence tools — primarily large language models (LLMs) such as those provided by Anthropic (Claude) and OpenAI — to automate tasks such as generating personalised written responses, categorising data, and drafting communications on behalf of our clients.

In line with the EU AI Act and the principle of transparency, we disclose the following:

AI is used as a tool, not a decision-maker. In all automations built by Sophfree Automation, AI-generated outputs are the result of instructions and parameters set by a human. Where AI generates content that is sent to a third party (for example, a personalised email response), the automation is designed and approved by the client business before deployment.

No high-risk AI systems. The automations we build do not fall within the categories of high-risk AI systems as defined in Annex III of the EU AI Act. We do not build systems that make consequential decisions about individuals in areas such as employment, creditworthiness, law enforcement, or access to essential services without human oversight.

Human oversight is maintained. Clients who commission automations from Sophfree Automation retain full control over and responsibility for the systems they operate after handover. We design all systems with clear points at which a human can review, override, or intervene.

AI-generated content disclosure. Where automations generate content that interacts with members of the public — such as personalised email responses — we recommend that our clients include appropriate disclosure where required by applicable law.

If you have questions about how AI is used in a specific automation or project, please contact sophie@sophfree.com.
